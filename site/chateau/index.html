    Welcome to
    
      The Chateau
      
    
      A haunted estate built for Mortez. Explore every room, and play the Reaper's game while you're here.
      
    
        
      
      1. ExploreThe grounds, the foyer, the haunted library, the Chapel, the merch shop, the Reaper Ride and more. Walk with WASD, drag to look, and press E to use things.Walk with the joystick, drag to look, and tap things to use them.
        2. 
      
      2. Find the six relicsThe Black Rose, the Scythe Blade, the Raven's Feather, the First Pressing, the Sum Fortis Ring and the Lost Lyric Page are hidden around the Chateau. Walk up to one to take it. Your count is at the top right.
        3. 
      
      3. Take the Reaper's KeyFind all six and the Reaper leaves his key beside his Ledger in the library. It opens the locked door upstairs in the foyer, marked Keep Out: the Reaper's Study.
        4. 
      
      4. Stuck? Read the LedgerThe Reaper's Ledger, at the far end of the library, gives you a hint each time you read it, and marks that relic on your map.
        5. 
    
      
    
      You're not alone in here. Other visitors walk the Chateau with you. Press T for emotes and Enter to chat.Tap 😈 for emotes.
